A little about me

Clarity is the common thread.

I’m Samuel Nii Amu Darko.

A full-stack developer, designer, and computing educator based in Accra, Ghana.

My work brings together software development, visual communication, and education. I build tools for business and learning, and create the visual materials that help people understand and use them.

More than a decade in the classroom has taught me to listen carefully, explain clearly, and make complex ideas approachable. Those habits are just as useful when designing a screen or planning an application.
