A little about me

Clarity is the common thread.

I’m Samuel Nii Amu Darko.

A full-stack developer and digital technology professional working across e-commerce, website management and digital marketing.

I build web applications and digital storefronts, then help businesses manage the content, customer journeys and day-to-day website operations behind them. My work includes product presentation, enquiry and checkout workflows, responsive interfaces and ongoing maintenance.

Alongside development, I plan marketing content, coordinate graphics and video, and work on search optimisation and customer acquisition. At Sellux Plaster Ltd, my work combines GA4 and Google Tag Manager enquiry tracking with product promotion, retail-partner prospecting and sales follow-up.

My BSc in Mathematics Education and more than a decade of teaching inform how I work: listen carefully, analyse a problem and explain the next step clearly. That background also supports my approach to data, client communication and business development.
