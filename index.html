  • Systems Architecture

    Feb 18, 2026

    Engineering Offline-First POS Systems in Ghana

    Why deterministic SQLite ledgers, idempotency keys, and asynchronous sync reconciliation are strictly mandatory for African retail.

    In Accra, assuming 100% network connectivity is an architectural failure before writing the first line of code. When a checkout counter has three customers waiting and cellular towers drop, the cash register must never freeze.

    1. Local-First Ledger with SQLite

    Every transaction in DwasoPOS is written locally first with a UUIDv4 idempotency key before attempting any cloud sync. The client UI marks the sale settled instantaneously.

    "Offline is not an error state; it is a normal operating condition."

    2. Background Reconciler with Conflict Resolution

    When connectivity resumes, transactions are batched to the Django backend. A PostgreSQL select_for_update lock ensures inventory counts are decremented deterministically without race conditions.

  • Mobile & Hardware

    Nov 28, 2025

    Sub-100ms Thermal Printing over Bluetooth in Flutter

    Direct binary byte protocol bridges for 58mm/80mm thermal printers over Android Bluetooth Low Energy without bloated third-party SDKs.

    Most retail thermal printers use the ESC/POS standard originally created by Epson. While heavy plugins try to render receipt bitmaps, generating raw byte arrays for text, bold tags, and paper cuts executes in mere milliseconds.

    Packet Chunking for BLE Stability

    Bluetooth Low Energy has an MTU limit (often 20 to 512 bytes). Chunking the ESC/POS byte buffer into 100-byte packets with small micro-delays guarantees printing never drops mid-receipt.
