The YAT Hub

A bespoke digital platform uniting three pillars — YAT Ministries (gospel music, worship, ministry bookings), YAT Finance (financial coaching, wealth strategy, book distribution) and YAT Foundation CIC (community empowerment, youth development, creative arts) — under one brand for a UK-based founder. The rebuild replaces a fragmented WordPress installation with a purpose-built Django application featuring 3D interactive elements, smooth scroll animations and a booking engine that serves all three pillars.

  • Django 5
  • HTMX
  • Three.js
  • GSAP
  • Lenis
  • PostgreSQL
